📖 Help & Controls

🎨 How to Paint

You can paint simultaneously on both the 2D UV canvas and directly onto the 3D character model!

🎮 3D Navigation Controls

  • Left Click + Drag: Paint directly onto the 3D model.
  • Right Click + Drag: Rotate the 3D camera around the character.
  • Mouse Scroll Wheel: Zoom in / out.
